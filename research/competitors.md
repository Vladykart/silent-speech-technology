# Competitors and adjacent organizations

**Purpose:** category map, not endorsement or exhaustive market scan. Status is frozen 2026-07-28 and should be re-verified before external use. “Research prototype,” “dataset,” “trial,” “developer kit,” and “shipping product” are not synonyms.

## Status taxonomy

- **Peer-reviewed research:** paper/system evaluated under reported conditions.
- **Open repository/dataset:** artifacts are accessible under stated terms; does not imply a product.
- **Clinical feasibility trial:** registered investigation; does not imply approval or availability.
- **Developer/consumer product:** orderable or access-controlled offering for its stated intended use; does not validate silent speech unless that is the stated tested use.
- **Company announcement/marketing:** establishes only what the company says.
- **Approved/cleared medical product:** requires exact regulator, indication, model, jurisdiction, and decision ID. None is claimed here for silent-speech decoding.

## Competitive wedge to test—not claim

The captain-selected wedge is e& frontline retail advisors and field technicians. It is not a better foundation model; it is a governed interaction layer for one to-be-selected workflow and a deliberately small command grammar:

- visible sensing state and consent;
- local-processing/raw-retention targets;
- candidates plus abstention and human confirmation;
- measurement of setup burden, cross-session drift, and alternative-input preference;
- adapter into an existing sandbox only after confirmation.

This becomes defensible only through consented evidence, placement/calibration know-how, reliability and failure operations, user-approved data rights, and workflow adoption. None exists yet.
